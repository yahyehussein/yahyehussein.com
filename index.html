Why I'm building it

Games are where a lot of children spend their free time, and too many game platforms weren't built with them in mind. I want a place where a game starts the moment you tap it, where safety and fair prices come first rather than last, and where anyone with an idea can make a game with their own AI, without years of learning to code.
